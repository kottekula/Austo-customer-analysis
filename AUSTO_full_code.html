Customer Analysis - AUSTO Dataset¶

The goal of this project is to analyze customer data to understand buying behavior and identifying factors influencing car purchase.

Problem Statement¶

The goal is to analyze customer characteristics such as age, income, profession, marital status, and loan status to understand car purchase behavior. The analysis aims to identify patterns influencing the choice of car type (Hatchback, Sedan, SUV) and provide business recommendations for the company.

There is a strong positive relationship between salary, partner salary, and total income. This shows that higher income leads to higher purchasing power for cars.

Key Insights¶

  • Higher income customers tend to buy more expensive cars
  • SUVs are generally priced higher than hatchbacks and sedans
  • Most customers fall in the middle-age group

Recommendations¶

  • Focus marketing on high-income customers
  • Promote SUVs as premium products
  • Provide loan and EMI options to attract more buyers
