# AUSTO Customer Analysis — Data Analytics Project

## 📌 Overview

This project analyzes customer data from the AUSTO dataset to understand customer characteristics, purchasing behavior, and preferences related to car purchases.

The analysis explores how factors such as age, income, profession, marital status, and loan status may relate to customers' car purchasing behavior and vehicle preferences.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze customer demographics and financial characteristics.
- Understand customer car purchasing behavior.
- Explore factors associated with car type preferences.
- Identify patterns in customer data.
- Generate actionable business insights.
- Provide recommendations to support data-driven marketing strategies.

## 📊 Project Workflow

1. **Data Loading:** Load and inspect the AUSTO customer dataset.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Examine missing values, duplicates, and data quality.
3. **Exploratory Data Analysis:** Analyze customer characteristics and purchasing patterns.
4. **Customer Segmentation:** Explore differences between customer groups.
5. **Feature Analysis:** Investigate relationships between customer attributes and car preferences.
6. **Business Insights:** Identify meaningful patterns in the data.
7. **Recommendations:** Suggest potential strategies based on the analysis.

## 🔍 Areas of Analysis

- Customer age and income
- Professional and marital status
- Loan status
- Car type preferences, including Hatchback, Sedan, and SUV
- Relationships between customer characteristics and purchasing behavior
